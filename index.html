01 · MY STORY

A journey from machines
to intelligent systems.

My story started with a simple interest in understanding how things work. That curiosity became a journey through mechanical systems, electronics, automation, robotics, software and data.

01
THE FOUNDATION

Learning how systems work

I studied Mechatronics Engineering at Ain Shams University, where I learned to look at engineering as one connected system rather than isolated disciplines.

Mechanical design, electronics, programming, control systems and automation became different pieces of the same picture.

02
BUILDING

Turning ideas into machines

During university, I started turning engineering concepts into physical systems. I worked with robotic arms, automated production lines, machine vision, PLCs, PID control, SolidWorks, MATLAB and Siemens TIA Portal.

This was where engineering stopped being only something I studied and became something I could actually build.

03
THE TURNING POINT

Building an autonomous robot

My graduation project brought everything together. I developed an Autonomous Shelf Lift Robot designed to navigate and operate autonomously in a warehouse environment.

I worked with ROS1, LiDAR, EKF-based sensor fusion, autonomous navigation, embedded systems and Gazebo, while also working with the physical robot and its charging and power systems.

A+ Graduation Project
04
EXPANDING

From hardware to software

As I worked on engineering systems, I became increasingly interested in the software behind them — not only how software is built, but how it behaves, how it fails and how it can be tested.

This led me toward software testing, Java, Python, APIs, Jira, Postman, Selenium and ISTQB-based testing practices.

05
DATA & AI

Learning to work with data

I also expanded my technical background into data analysis and artificial intelligence, working with Python, SQL, NumPy, Pandas, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow and OpenCV.

This added another perspective to the way I approach engineering problems: understanding systems through both physical behavior and data.

06
REAL-WORLD ENGINEERING

Working with real operations

My professional experience has also taken me beyond pure engineering development.

At Delotrans, I worked in logistics operations, compliance and coordination. Today, as a Technical Office Engineer at Orascom Services, I work with Volvo Penta engine operations, spare-parts identification, technical documentation and requirements.

These experiences taught me that engineering is not only about designing something that works. It is also about communication, accuracy, documentation, responsibility and solving real operational problems.

07
TODAY

Still building. Still learning.

Today, my interests sit at the intersection of engineering and technology — robotics, automation, embedded systems, software testing, data and intelligent systems.

I am continuing to develop across these areas because I believe the strongest solutions come from understanding the complete system, not just one part of it.
