01 · ABOUT

01

Where it started

I graduated from Ain Shams University with a degree in Mechatronics Engineering, a field that brought together the areas I enjoyed most: mechanical systems, electronics, control and programming.

02

Building real systems

During university, I moved from studying individual engineering disciplines to building complete systems. My graduation project was an Autonomous Shelf Lift Robot, where I worked with ROS, LiDAR, sensor fusion, autonomous navigation, embedded systems and Gazebo. The project achieved an A+ grade.

03

Beyond robotics

My engineering work expanded into industrial automation and machine-based systems. I worked on automated production lines, robotic arms, machine vision, PLCs, PID control, SolidWorks, MATLAB and Siemens TIA Portal.

04

Moving into software

I gradually expanded my focus toward software and data-driven engineering. I developed skills in Python, SQL, data analysis, machine learning, deep learning, TensorFlow, OpenCV and Scikit-learn, while also building a foundation in software testing and quality assurance.

05

Engineering in the real world

Today, I work as a Technical Office Engineer in Engines & Spare Parts at Orascom Services, supporting Volvo Penta engine operations. My work includes spare-parts identification, technical documentation, requirements preparation and support for maintenance and service activities.

06

The way I work

My background has taught me to look at a problem as a complete system rather than as a single component. I combine engineering fundamentals, programming, analysis and practical problem-solving to understand how different parts work together and turn ideas into working solutions.
