01 · ABOUT

One engineer.
Many systems.

I started with machines — learning how mechanical and electrical systems work, how they fail, and how they can be improved.

At Ain Shams University, Mechatronics Engineering gave me the opportunity to bring those worlds together. I built systems that moved, sensed, communicated and responded.

My graduation project, an Autonomous Shelf Lift Robot, took that further into autonomous navigation, LiDAR, sensor fusion and real-world robotics. The project received an A+ grade.

From there, my interests expanded beyond the physical machine. I explored industrial automation, PLCs, robotic systems, embedded programming, machine vision and control — while also moving into Python, data analysis and machine learning.

My AI/ML experience gave me the opportunity to work with real datasets and build practical models, recommendation systems and data-driven solutions.

Then I discovered another side of engineering: making sure systems actually work.

Today, I am developing my software testing expertise through ISTQB-aligned training, test analysis, Jira, Postman and Selenium, while continuing to build on my engineering background.

At the same time, my current role at Orascom Services connects me back to physical engineering, where I work with Volvo Penta engine documentation, components and spare-parts requirements.

MY APPROACH

I don't see hardware, software, data and engineering as separate worlds. I enjoy understanding the whole system — from how it is designed, to how it operates, to how it can be made better.
